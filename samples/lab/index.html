The engines and models

Google Chirp 3 HD free

Google Cloud Text-to-Speech

Google's realistic voices (30 voices, the same names as Gemini: Leda, Puck, Charon…). Very natural, but each voice has one fixed personality: you can change the speed, not the age, mood or accent.

Runs on
Google's servers, called by the GitHub workflow with your key GOOGLE_TTS_KEY.
Price
First 1 million characters every month free, then $30 per million characters.
In your app
Used now for Hana (8% slower), Yuki, Kenta, Ren, Sato-sensei, Hayato, Keiko, you and the narrator.

VOICEVOX free

Free, open source (Japan)

A Japanese text-to-speech program with ~45 characters voiced by real voice actors, many styles each (normal, happy, whisper…). The only free engine with convincing kids and old men. A bit anime-like next to Google's voices.

Runs on
Inside the GitHub workflow (a Docker container), nothing to pay. GitHub Actions is free for public repos.
Price
Free, including commercial use. Each character's terms require a credit line (“VOICEVOX:name”), shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.
In your app
Used now for Sōta (白上虎太郎, ふつう), Tanaka-san (ちび式じい) and Mio (春日部つむぎ).

Google Gemini-TTS pay per use

Directed voices

The same 30 Google voices, but the model reads a description of the speaker before reading the line (“a 24-year-old from Osaka, strong Kansai accent, teasing, fast”). So it can act: age, mood, accent, pace. Each reading comes out a little different.

Runs on
Google's servers. Through the Gemini API with your key GEMINI_API_KEY (AI Studio).
Price
Gemini 2.5 Flash TTS: $0.50 per million text tokens + $10 per million audio tokens (25 tokens = 1 s), about $0.015 per minute of speech. Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS (preview) and 2.5 Pro TTS: about $0.03 per minute. Free tier on an AI Studio key without billing: 10 clips a day, too few for daily lessons.
In your app
Not used in the app yet. Tested here on the free tier (cards marked “directed”).

Google Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS / Flash-Lite TTS pay per use

Newest (Sept 2026) · voice design

Google's newest speech models. Besides directing a voice, they can DESIGN a brand-new voice from a description (age, timbre, accent) and keep it as a reusable voice id, and they have a library of 115 native Japanese voices with age and accent (Tokyo, Osaka, Fukuoka; ages 21–74, no children). Flash = best acting; Flash-Lite = cheaper and faster. Google refuses to design voices of minors, so Sōta and Mio can't get one.

Runs on
Google's servers, through the Gemini API with your key GEMINI_API_KEY.
Price
Flash TTS: $0.50 per million text tokens + $9 per million audio tokens (≈ $0.0135 per minute) until 31 Dec 2026, then $1 + $18 (≈ $0.027/min). Flash-Lite TTS: $0.50 + $6 (≈ $0.009/min), then $1 + $12 (≈ $0.018/min). Free tier without billing: 10 clips a day per model.
In your app
Not used in the app yet. Designed voices for 9 characters are stored in your Google project (voices38.json); samples are in each character's comparison below, marked “3.8”.

AivisSpeech + AivisHub free

Free, open source (Japan)

A newer free Japanese engine with very natural voices made by the community (AivisHub has 77). Scanned all of them: no child or grandmother, a few middle-aged men.

Runs on
Inside the GitHub workflow, like VOICEVOX.
Price
Free. Each model has its own licence.
In your app
Not used. Every voice is on the audition page.
